Deagen Fairclough, on starting in cars

Deagen grew up watching Formula 1 with his dad. At five, a visit to a local karting track did the rest. At 13 he moved into cars, racing Junior Saloons and then a Ford Fiesta in the BRSCC Junior series.

In May 2022 he won the ROKiT Racing Star F4 Esports Competition. The prize was a fully funded British F4 seat for 2023. “It changed my life,” he says.

He finished third in his first F4 season. In his second he won the title at Silverstone with five races to spare, taking a record 14 wins. Autosport and the BRDC named him Young Driver of the Year, alongside names like Coulthard, Button, Russell and Norris on the roll of honour.

Since then: two seasons in GB3 with Hitech, and a September 2025 drive in an Aston Martin AMR23 Formula 1 car at Silverstone.
